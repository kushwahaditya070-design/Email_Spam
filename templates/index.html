01 — THE IDEA

Not every email
deserves your attention.

Spam can be noisy, deceptive, and occasionally dangerous. Postmark uses a trained text-classification model to spot patterns that distinguish unwanted messages from ordinary email.

It's a learning project, not a replacement for your email provider's security filters. Always treat unexpected links and attachments with care.
